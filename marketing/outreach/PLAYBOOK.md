# JISR Arabic outreach playbook

The `jisr-outreach` agent (`.claude/agents/jisr-outreach.md`) reads this file before every task.
It prospects through the ProspectHalo connector (connect it in claude.ai connector settings).
Edit it to change who we contact, what we offer and how we sound. Facts the agent may state about
JISR Arabic come **only** from this file; anything marked **TO CONFIRM** is never sent until you fill it in.

## Product (facts the agent may use)

- **What it is:** JISR Arabic (جِسْر), an online Arabic learning app at https://jisrarabic.com, app at https://app.jisrarabic.com.
- **Who it's for:** children in UAE schools learning Arabic as an additional language under the **CBSE curriculum, Classes 1 to 10**, and parents who help with homework without speaking Arabic.
- **Built on the school textbook:** the CBSE Arabic textbook pages, line by line, term by term, so home practice matches class.
- **Features:**
  - line-by-line English for every textbook line;
  - native audio for every word at normal, 0.6× and 0.3× speed;
  - **Ask Fahim**, an AI tutor that explains in English, answers in Arabic and reads aloud;
  - an exam paper assistant;
  - mind maps, printable study booklets and CBSE-format timed exam practice;
  - a mastery map, ranks and badges.
- **Access:** any modern browser on a phone, tablet or computer, with nothing to install. Each child signs in with the parent's email and a 4-digit PIN.
- **Parent pricing:**
  - Chapter 1 of Term 1 is free (no card needed);
  - **$10 per term per child**;
  - **$25.50 per full year per child** (15% off $30);
  - payments by Stripe, with a UAE tax invoice for every payment.
- **Schools:** JISR supports **school voucher / licence codes**, so a school can sponsor access for its students (students redeem a code instead of paying).

## Who we contact (B2B only)

1. **CBSE-curriculum schools in the UAE**: Principal, Vice Principal, Head of Arabic / Head of Languages, Academic Coordinator, Head of Primary/Middle School.
2. **Tutoring and learning centres** in the UAE teaching CBSE Arabic.
3. **School groups and education networks** running CBSE schools in the UAE.

Never contact individual parents or students, anyone under 18, or anyone who has opted out.

## Tone

- Warm, brief and specific: about 120 words for a first email, and one clear ask (a 15-minute demo or a free pilot).
- Lead with the school's problem: students without Arabic at home struggle with homework, and parents can't help.
- Personalise from real facts about the school (curriculum, size, location). Never invent facts.
- English by default; offer an Arabic version when the contact's role suggests it (e.g. Head of Arabic).
- No pressure tactics, false urgency or exaggerated claims (e.g. no "guaranteed grades").
