# JISR Arabic outreach playbook

The `jisr-outreach` agent (`.claude/agents/jisr-outreach.md`) reads this file before every task.
It prospects through the ProspectHalo connector (connect it in claude.ai connector settings).
Edit it to change who we contact, what we offer and how we sound. Facts the agent may state about
JISR Arabic come **only** from this file; anything marked **TO CONFIRM** is never sent until you fill it in.

## Product (facts the agent may use)

- **What it is:** JISR Arabic (جِسْر), an online Arabic learning app at https://jisrarabic.com, app at https://app.jisrarabic.com.
- **Who it's for:** children in UAE schools learning Arabic as an additional language under the **CBSE curriculum, Classes 1 to 10**, and parents who help with homework without speaking Arabic.
- **Built on the school textbook:** the CBSE Arabic textbook pages, line by line, term by term, so home practice matches class.
- **Features:**
  - line-by-line English for every textbook line;
  - native audio for every word at normal, 0.6× and 0.3× speed;
  - **Ask Fahim**, an AI tutor that explains in English, answers in Arabic and reads aloud;
  - an exam paper assistant;
  - mind maps, printable study booklets and CBSE-format timed exam practice;
  - a mastery map, ranks and badges.
- **Access:** any modern browser on a phone, tablet or computer, with nothing to install. Each child signs in with the parent's email and a 4-digit PIN.
- **Parent pricing:**
  - Chapter 1 of Term 1 is free (no card needed);
  - **$10 per term per child**;
  - **$25.50 per full year per child** (15% off $30);
  - payments by Stripe, with a UAE tax invoice for every payment.
- **Schools:** JISR supports **school voucher / licence codes**, so a school can sponsor access for its students (students redeem a code instead of paying).

## Who we contact

There are two audiences, each with its own rules. Never contact students, anyone under 18, or anyone who has
opted out.

### A. Schools and organisations (B2B: email or LinkedIn)

1. **CBSE-curriculum schools in the UAE**: Principal, Vice Principal, Head of Arabic / Head of Languages, Academic Coordinator, Head of Primary/Middle School.
2. **Tutoring and learning centres** in the UAE teaching CBSE Arabic.
3. **School groups and education networks** running CBSE schools in the UAE.

### B. Parents (LinkedIn only)

Parents in the UAE whose children (Grades/Classes 1–10) learn Arabic at a CBSE school, especially parents who
can't help with Arabic homework themselves. ProspectHalo agent: **"JISR Arabic – UAE CBSE parents"** (LinkedIn
only, every message reviewed before sending).

- **LinkedIn only:** contact parents through their own LinkedIn profile, from the JISR ARABIC LinkedIn account. Never use personal email addresses or phone numbers, and never contact them through their employer.
- **Adults only:** never contact a student or anyone who appears to be under 18.
- **Don't assume they're a parent.** LinkedIn rarely confirms it, so write "if your children learn Arabic at a CBSE school…". Never claim to know about their children, school or grades.
- **No nationality or ethnicity in messages.** Being an Indian expat is a targeting preference only and must never be mentioned or implied.
- **Respect "no" immediately.** Make it easy to say no, and never follow up after a decline, no reply to the full sequence, or a request to stop.

## Offer for parents (ready to use)

- **The ask:** try **Chapter 1 of Term 1 free**, no card needed, at https://app.jisrarabic.com.
- **Prices if asked:** $10 per term per child, or $25.50 per full year per child (15% off). Payments are by Stripe, with a UAE tax invoice.
- **Proof points:** the CBSE Arabic textbook line by line, English under every line, native audio at normal, 0.6× and 0.3× speed, and the Ask Fahim AI tutor for homework.

## Tone

- **Schools:** warm, brief and specific. Write about 120 words for a first email, with one clear ask (a 15-minute demo or a free pilot).
  - Lead with the school's problem: students without Arabic at home struggle with homework, and parents can't help.
  - Personalise from real facts about the school (curriculum, size, location).
  - Offer an Arabic version when the contact's role suggests it (e.g. Head of Arabic).
- **Parents:** specific and evidence-led.
  - **Connection note:** one or two sentences, with no pitch and no link. Say who you are and why it may be relevant (e.g. CBSE Arabic homework).
  - **Messages:** under 80 words each, with one clear ask (try the free first chapter). Lead with a concrete feature, not adjectives.
- **Always:** English by default, and never invent facts. No pressure tactics, false urgency or exaggerated claims (e.g. no "guaranteed grades").
